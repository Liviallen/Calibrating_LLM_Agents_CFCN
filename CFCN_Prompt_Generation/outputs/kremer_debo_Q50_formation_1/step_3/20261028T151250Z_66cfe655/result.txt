## 2. Validated context gaps

### Manipulated variables and experimentally-varied design elements
- The task is repeated across multiple rounds/products, not a one-shot choice: there are **26 payoff-relevant rounds**, each with a new product.
- Product value/quality is **newly drawn each round** for that round’s product.
- The realized wait-time quote is **endogenous within a round**: it depends on how many earlier arrivals in that round chose to order.
- Information status (“informed” vs. “uninformed”) is **round-specific**, not a permanent participant trait.
- In **Q50**, being informed means a **50% chance for a given subject in a given round**; it does **not** mean exactly two of the four consumers are informed each round.

### Role structure
- All human participants are **consumers only**. No human participant plays the firm; the firm/production process is part of the computerized environment.
- A subject’s only decision when called in a round is **whether to order or not order**. There are no pricing, production, or communication choices.

### Information structure
- Product quality is a **single common round-level state**: if the product is high value or low value in a round, that same value applies to all four consumers in that round.
- Information status is determined **separately for each subject in each round** and revealed to that subject.
- Informed subjects observe the **true realized product value before deciding**; uninformed subjects do not know it when deciding.
- Subjects do **not** observe their exact arrival rank when deciding.
- Wait time reveals the **number of prior orders**, not the number of prior arrivals: a quote of \(X\) weeks implies exactly \(X-1\) earlier orders, but there may have been additional earlier arrivals who chose not to order.
- Subjects have access to a **history box** showing past-round information, including prior wait times, consumer type/status, decisions, and realized product values.

### Timing and sequence
- Each round starts with a **new product** and fresh round-specific draws before any decisions are made.
- Within a round, **product quality is drawn before decisions**, and subjects’ information statuses are determined before decisions.
- The four consumers **arrive in random order** within the round, and each decides upon arrival after seeing the quoted wait time and her own information.
- **Production starts only after all four order decisions are collected.**
- Delivery then occurs **in order of purchase arrival**, at a rate of **one unit per week**.
- After the round’s decisions and delivery simulation, that round ends and the next **independent** product round begins.
- The experiment included **training rounds before the 26 payoff-relevant rounds**.

### Matching and memory
- Subjects are placed in **fixed cohorts of four** and stay with the same cohort across rounds.
- Despite fixed cohorts, rounds are otherwise **independent in their state variables**: queue/order history, product quality, arrival order, and information realizations reset each round.
- Past outcomes do **not mechanically carry over** into future-round queues or current-round wait times.
- Subjects may nevertheless **remember or consult visible history** from previous rounds.

### Incentives and payoffs
- The **$4 endowment is received anew each round**.
- Each round’s payoff depends only on that round’s **order decision, realized product value, and quoted wait time**.
- Earlier consumers’ order decisions can **indirectly affect later consumers’ payoffs** by increasing later consumers’ quoted wait times.
- Final payment is based on **average earnings across the 26 payoff-relevant rounds**.

### Randomization and assignment
- Each round re-randomizes the product’s realized value according to the known prior for that condition.
- Arrival order is **randomly redrawn each round**.
- A **random delay before the first arrival** is used so subjects cannot infer arrival rank from clock timing alone.
- The key stochastic elements that reset each round are **product value, each subject’s information status, and arrival order**.
