## 2. Candidate context gaps

### Manipulated variables and experimentally-varied design elements
- **Product quality is newly drawn for each new product/round** as either high or low.
- **Quoted wait time is endogenous within the round**: it depends on how many earlier arrivals chose to purchase, not on a fixed product attribute.
- **“Informed” vs. “uninformed” is a round-specific state**, not a permanent subject type.

### Role structure
- **All human participants are buyers/consumers only**; there is no human seller role making strategic decisions.
- **The only active decision for a subject when called is whether to purchase or not purchase.**  
  - This is mostly stated already, so likely low priority unless included for completeness.

### Information structure
- **Information status is privately determined separately for each subject for each round.**
- **Product quality is a single common round-level state** shared by all four subjects; informed subjects who are informed about quality observe that same realized state.
- **Wait time is informative because it reflects cumulative prior purchases** by earlier arrivals.
- **Subjects have access to a history of past rounds**, including prior wait times, own/others’ type information as displayed, decisions, and realized product values.  
  - Keep only if the context prompt is meant to cover repeated-round memory/interface.

### Timing and sequence
- **The task consists of 26 rounds/products.**
- **Each round begins with a new product and a fresh draw of round-specific variables.**
- **Within a round, product quality is drawn before any purchase decisions are made.**
- **After all four decisions are made, production/delivery is carried out/simulated; this does not involve further decisions.**
- **After a round ends, the next round starts with a new independent product.**

### Matching and memory
- **Subjects remain in fixed cohorts of four across rounds (partner matching).**
- **Despite fixed cohorts, rounds are independent in the sense that queue, quality, arrival order, and information draws reset each round.**
- **Past outcomes do not carry over mechanically into later-round queues or payoffs.**
- **Visible history from past rounds may be available to subjects.**

### Incentives and payoffs
- **The $4 endowment is refreshed each round**, not just once for the whole experiment.
- **Final payment is based on average earnings across all 26 rounds**, so each round contributes to final compensation.
- **Earlier subjects’ purchase decisions can indirectly affect one’s payoff** by changing the wait time one faces.

### Randomization and assignment
- **Product quality is redrawn each round according to the publicly known prior shown for that round.**
- **In Q50, informed/uninformed status is redrawn for each subject each round with 50% probability of being informed.**
- **A random delay before the first arrival prevents subjects from inferring their arrival rank from clock timing alone.**
- **Quality, information status, and arrival order are all re-randomized each round.**
