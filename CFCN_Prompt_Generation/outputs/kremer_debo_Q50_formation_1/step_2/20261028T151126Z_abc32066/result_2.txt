## 2. Candidate context gaps

### Manipulated variables and experimentally-varied design elements
- **Product quality varies across rounds and is newly drawn for each new product.**
- **“Informed” vs. “uninformed” is a round-specific state rather than a permanent subject role.**

### Role structure
- **All human participants are buyers/consumers; there is no separate participant playing the firm/seller role.**

### Information structure
- **Whether a subject is informed is determined separately for each subject in each round and revealed privately to that subject.**
- **The realized product quality is a single common round-level state shared by all four subjects in that round.**
- **Subjects do not know their exact rank in the arrival sequence when deciding.**
- **Subjects have access to a history of past rounds, including prior wait times, information status, decisions, and realized product values.**

### Timing and sequence
- **The experiment consists of 26 rounds, each corresponding to a new product.**
- **After all four decisions in a round, the round ends and the next round begins with a new independent product and fresh round-specific draws.**

### Matching and memory
- **Subjects are placed in fixed cohorts of 4 and remain with the same cohort across rounds.**
- **Despite fixed cohorts, each round is otherwise independent: the new round has a new product, new quality draw, new arrival order, and new information-status realizations.**
- **Past outcomes do not carry over mechanically into later rounds’ queues or payoffs.**
- **Past-round information remains visible in a history box.**

### Incentives and payoffs
- **The $4 endowment is refreshed each round.**
- **Final payment is based on average earnings across the 26 rounds, so each round contributes to total compensation.**

### Randomization and assignment
- **Product quality is randomly redrawn each round according to the known prior for that round.**
- **In Q50, informed/uninformed status is randomly redrawn for each subject each round with 50% probability of being informed.**
- **A random delay is added before the first arrival so subjects cannot infer rank from timing alone.**
- **Quality, information status, and arrival order are all redrawn each round.**
